## I. Project Attributes

**a.** Explain why the project attributes time, cost and scope are interdependent.

**b.** Suppose your team has been asked to develop a project within three months and you are told that the project scope cannot be reduced. What options are open to you as the project manager of the team?

**c.** Consider the experience of Jack.

> During Jack's six-month internship he was asked to work on a project to deliver a new piece of software. After a couple of weeks, the project sponsor decided to add some new requirements and Jack happily included them. Two weeks later, the sponsor made some more changes and asked for some new functionality. Again, Jack said that it was no problem. The changes were made. Towards the end of the three months, the sponsor complained that Jack was behind schedule. Jack tried to explain that all the changes meant that there was no way that the software could have been completed to the original timescales. The sponsor was not happy, and Jack was labeled as being "too slow".

What caused the problem above? How could Jack have avoided this?
