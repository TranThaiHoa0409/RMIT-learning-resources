## 2. AI Policy Making: How-To

### What is an AI Policy?
A set of rules for an organization to:
- Set expectations for staff
- Support consistent decision-making
- Align AI use with legal obligations

**Formula:** `AI Policy = Ethics + Laws in AI`

### Template
- **Source:** Australian National AI Center — provides a complete sample policy usable as-is or adaptable.

### Pre-publish checklist
Before publishing an AI policy, make sure it clearly covers:
- [ ] What AI can and cannot be used for
- [ ] Who approves higher-risk use cases
- [ ] What data staff may input into AI tools
- [ ] How staff report issues/misuse of AI tools
- [ ] When the policy will be reviewed

## 3. In-class Exercise — Create Your Startup's AI Policy

**Task:** Design an AI Policy applying the Golden Rule and the Vietnam AI Law.
**Group size:** 4+ students

**Scenario:** You are the leadership board of an AI startup close to securing a USD 1M investment. A key investor requirement: the company must have a practical, ethical AI Policy that complies with:
- the ethical foundation of the **Golden Rule**, and
- the **Vietnam AI Law**.

**Steps:**
1. *(5 min)* Fill in all `[placeholders]` in the template (6 placeholders total, e.g., `[Date of last update to the policy]`, `[Organisation Name]`).
2. *(25 min)* Select and modify **at least 2 points** in the policy to align with:
   - The Golden Rule
   - The Vietnam AI Law (cite the specific Chapter/Article referenced)
3. Present the policy to the class and collect feedback.

> 💡 This maps directly onto the AI ethics policy assignment (Option 6 — transparency & contestability): use the checklist above as a structural guide, and ground any transparency/contestability clauses in a specific Chapter/Article of Law No. 134/2025/QH15.
