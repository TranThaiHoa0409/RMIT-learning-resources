## 1. Introduce Yourself

**Purpose:** This course includes team activities & group assignments → help classmates get to know each other.

**How to do it:**
- Students have 5 minutes to prepare their introduction.
- Each student gives a short introduction (max 1 min).

**Suggested info to include:**
- Your name (and what you'd like others to call you).
- Where you're from, your major/minor.
- What you expect from this course, or your interest in AI.
- Personal hobbies or anything else you'd like to share — this may help you find teammates more easily.

## 2. Machine Learning vs Deep Learning vs LLM & Types of AI

**Activity:**
- Watch two videos on the following topics:
  1. Machine Learning vs Deep Learning vs LLM (by **IBM Technology**)
  2. Types of AI (by **IBM Technology**)
- Each video can be played twice.
- Take notes, then share and discuss with a classmate sitting next to you.
- A Kahoot quiz is included to reinforce the concepts (link on the slide).

> 💡 **Review tip:** This exercise trains you to distinguish "what is actually AI" from "what is just fixed programmed logic / simple automation." Ask yourself: does the system **learn from data** and **adapt**, or does it just follow pre-programmed rules (rule-based)?

**Types of AI to know** (common classifications — by capability and by functionality):
- By capability: Narrow AI (ANI), General AI (AGI), Super AI (ASI).
- By functionality: Reactive Machines, Limited Memory, Theory of Mind, Self-Aware.

## 3. [Bonus] How To Take Notes Effectively

**Activity:** Watch the video "Note Taking Method" by **Jim Kwik**, and count how many key points appear in the video while watching.

- After watching, discuss: how many key points were there in the video?
- Accompanying notebook/note-taking images are shown on the slides.

> 💡 **Review tip:** This is a supplementary soft skill — active note-taking helps you learn more effectively in a course with many videos and group discussions.

## ✅ Key takeaways for review
- Clearly distinguish: **Machine Learning** ⊂ **AI**; **Deep Learning** ⊂ **ML**; **LLM** (Large Language Model) is an application of Deep Learning.
- Be able to classify everyday AI examples and explain the reasoning.
- Know the classifications of AI (by capability and by functionality).
- Remember the effective note-taking skills (bonus).
