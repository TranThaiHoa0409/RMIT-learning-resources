### 2.2 Activities

1. [5 mins] Tutor divides class into 3–4 groups.
2. [15 mins] Pick an (existing or imagined) AI application you want to share. Discuss the following questions among your group:
   - What is the initial problem?
   - How does AI specifically address the problem? What type(s)/algorithm(s) of AI have been used in the app (e.g., machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, generative AI, limited memory, reactive machine, ...)?
   - Are there any issues or pitfalls associated with using AI in this application?
3. [15 mins] Prepare a 3-minute slide or infographic using [canva.com](https://canva.com), then post the link to this padlet: https://rmit.padlet.org/binhnguyen49/fascinating-ai-applications-worldwide-xuwg30bww55mpe68
4. [5 mins per group] Present and Q&A.

### 2.3 Example: Self-Driving Cars

**Description of the application:** True self-driving cars (level 5) let the AI drive the car entirely on its own (no human assistance during the driving task at any time).

- **Initial problem:** Safety, transportation cost-effectiveness, accessibility for disabled people.
- **How does AI address the problem:** AI acts as the brain of the car by processing environmental data collected by sensors such as radars and cameras. The AI performs all driving tasks such as lane keeping, accelerating, decelerating, maintaining distance, and stopping. It uses many types of AI algorithms: deep learning, reinforcement learning, generative AI…
- **Pitfalls:** Job loss for drivers, high car prices, responsibility issues (determining guilt in accidents), threat of terrorist/hacker misuse.
