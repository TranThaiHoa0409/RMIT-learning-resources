## 2. AI Policy Making: How-To

An AI policy is a set of rules for your organization to:

- Set expectations for staff.
- Support consistent decision-making.
- Align AI use with legal obligations.

→ In short, AI policy = Ethics + Laws in AI.

Use the template for AI Policy: [Australian National AI Center](https://www.ai.gov.au/staying-safe-and-responsible/essential-ai-practices/create-ai-policy). 

- It gives you a structure and examples to get started.
- Note: This template is, in fact, a complete sample policy. You can use it as is or modify any part of it to better suit how your organisation operates.

**Checklist:** Before publishing your AI policy, ensure it clearly outlines:

- What AI can & cannot be used for.
- Who approves higher-risk use cases.
- What data staff are allowed to input into AI tools.
- How staff should report issues or misuse of AI tools.
- When the policy will be reviewed.

This ensures the policy is practical and actionable for day-to-day work.
