## 2. Card Game: Machine Learning Types

### Activities

Work in groups of 4–5 students. The tutor will give cards to each group.

1. **[10 mins]** Review with your teammates: What defines each type of ML? — Classification, Regression, Clustering, Reinforcement Learning.
2. **[15 mins]** Sort your team cards into the 4 ML types. Discuss and write a brief explanation for each card: why does the card fit its type?
3. **[15 mins]** Pick 1 example from each category to share with the class.

## 3. Assignment 1 Preparation

- Read the assignment details on Canvas. If you have any questions, consult your tutor.
- Research the following AI problems: **Automation Bias**, **AI Sycophancy**, and **AI-associated Delusions**. Feel free to discuss with your classmates and/or consult your tutor.
- Choose one problem and research the AI limitations (both in AI development and AI usage) that contribute to the problem. Again, feel free to discuss with your classmates or consult your tutor if needed.
