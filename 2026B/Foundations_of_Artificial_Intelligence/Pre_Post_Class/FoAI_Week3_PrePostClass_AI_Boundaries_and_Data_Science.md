# Week 3: AI Boundaries and Data Science

## 1. AI Boundaries and Future Directions

### Food for Thought

Examining the boundary between human and AI capabilities requires first considering what defines humanity — candidate criteria include emotions, the capacity for moral decision-making, and consciousness.

A classical philosophical reference point for this question is Descartes' statement, "I think, therefore I am" (Descartes, 1637). If thinking is taken as the defining feature of being human, this raises the open question of whether AI can be said to truly think.

## 2. Data Science Basics

### What is Data?

> Data is a collection of discrete or continuous values that convey information, or simply sequences of symbols that may be further interpreted formally. (Wikipedia)

In this context, "interpreted formally" means treating data as input for a systematic process — using statistical formulas to identify patterns or trends.

### Types of Data

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Tabular | CSV, Excel files with numbers and categories |
| Textual | Tweets, emails |
| Audio | Podcasts, voice commands |
| Visual | Images, videos |

### What is Data Science?

> Data science combines maths and statistics, specialised programming, AI and machine learning to uncover insights hidden in an organisation's data. (IBM)

In short, data science is about extracting insights and knowledge from data.

### Data and AI Systems

Deep learning and other data-intensive AI systems require large amounts of data and computing power. Data science provides the methods needed to manage and derive insight from the massive, often unstructured, datasets used to train such systems.
