## Overview
Individually design and build a simple Android application using **Kotlin** and modern Android development tools. The app should demonstrate understanding of key Android components, UI design principles, and app architecture while solving a meaningful, practical problem.

## Objectives — Your app must:
- Be built using Kotlin, following modern development practices (MVVM pattern, Clean Architecture, Jetpack Compose)
- Include at least **3 screens/activities** or composable navigation destinations
- Include at least **one meaningful model class** to represent your data
- Accomplish a reasonable and meaningful task
- Be creative, engaging, and easy to use

## Technical Requirements
- Use of **Jetpack Compose** for UI design
- Integration of at least **one RESTful API** (e.g., for content, images, or data)
- Use of **Google Maps API** or location services to enhance app functionality
- Use of a **database** (e.g., Room, Firebase) to store user data or app content
- Proper navigation between screens using Compose Navigation or Activities

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **Code quality:** proper error handling (try/catch), null checks, clean architecture
- **Architecture:** implement MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
- **Consistent UI:** harmony in fonts, colors, and images across screens
- **Usability:** easy navigation with minimal clicks to access functionalities
- **Personalization:** custom app icon and a splash screen

## App Ideas (choose one, or propose your own — email for approval)

1. **Tourism & Culture** — Platform for locals/travelers to discover and share hidden alleys, rooftop cafes, or street murals off the beaten path.
2. **Gaming & Fitness** — Real-world gamified RPG where walking through historic districts (e.g., Hanoi's Old Quarter, Independence Palace) unlocks history chapters and cultural trivia.
3. **Public Health & Food Safety** — Crowdsourced directory rating street food vendors on hygiene, ingredient freshness, and cooking standards.
4. **Transportation & Urban Logistics** — Live tracking hub for mobile street vendors (bánh mì, fruit, chè) so residents know when their favorite seller is nearby.
5. **Education & Campus Life** — Campus companion app for university hubs to coordinate shared rides, find budget meals, and track open study spots.
6. **Environmental Sustainability** — Maps active e-waste/battery recycling boxes across supermarkets and schools, with eco-point rewards.
7. **Social & Community Service** — Seasonal safety/rescue app for heavy rains: report flooded streets, flag broken-down motorbikes, connect riders with nearby mechanics.
8. **Gig Economy & Freelance** — Rest-stop locator for delivery riders/xe ôm drivers: driver-friendly rest zones, restrooms, cheap cơm tấm spots with free parking and trà đá.
9. **Productivity & Real Estate** — Tool to discover/filter local cafes by remote-work amenities (Wi-Fi speed, power outlets, quiet areas).
10. **Agriculture & E-Commerce** — Direct B2C marketplace connecting urban buyers/small businesses with regional farms (e.g., Mekong Delta orchards, Da Lat coffee), cutting out middlemen.
