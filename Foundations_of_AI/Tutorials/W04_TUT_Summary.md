## 2. Card Game: Machine Learning Types

### Activity
- Work in groups of 4–5 students. The tutor will give cards to each group.
- **[10 mins]** Review with your teammates: **What defines each type of ML?**
  - **Classification**
  - **Regression**
  - **Clustering**
  - **Reinforcement Learning**
- **[15 mins]** Sort your team's cards into the 4 ML types above. Discuss and write a brief explanation for each card: **why does the card fit its type?**
- **[15 mins]** Pick 1 example from each category to share with the class.

> 💡 **Review tip — the 4 basic Machine Learning types:**
> - **Classification:** predicts discrete labels/classes (e.g., spam vs. not spam).
> - **Regression:** predicts a continuous numeric value (e.g., predicting house prices).
> - **Clustering:** groups unlabeled data into clusters with similar characteristics (unsupervised learning).
> - **Reinforcement Learning:** an agent learns through trial and error, receiving rewards/penalties from the environment to optimize its actions.

## 3. Assignment 1 Preparation

### Preparation Steps
- Read the assignment details on **Canvas**; if you have any questions, consult your tutor.
- Research the following AI problems:
  - **Automation Bias**
  - **AI Sycophancy**
  - **AI-associated Delusions**
  - Feel free to discuss with your classmates and/or consult your tutor.
- Choose **one problem** and research the **AI limitations** (both in AI development and AI usage) that contribute to that problem.
  - Again, feel free to discuss with your classmates or consult your tutor if needed.

> 💡 **Extra explanation for review of these terms:**
> - **Automation Bias:** the tendency for humans to over-trust the decisions/suggestions of an automated (AI) system, even when it is wrong.
> - **AI Sycophancy:** the tendency of AI to agree with or tell users what they want to hear, rather than giving objective/accurate answers.
> - **AI-associated Delusions:** a condition where users form false beliefs/delusions due to excessive interaction with, or blind trust in, AI.
