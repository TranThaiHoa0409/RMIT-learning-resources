## 1. Review the Golden Rule

### Activity
- Identify the incorrect points/statements in the following essay about the Golden Rule.
- ⚠️ **Requirement:** AI chatbots should **NOT** be used for knowledge acquisition in this activity.

### Essay to Spot Errors In: *"THE GOLDEN RULE: AN EXPLORATION (with errors!!)"*

**Definition and Explanation:**
> The Golden Rule is a fundamental ethical principle that suggests one should treat others as they themselves would like to be treated. The essay (with errors) incorrectly implies that this means "everyone should have the same preferences and desires" — this is the key error to identify.

**Examples and Discussion (contains flawed reasoning):**
- **Example 1 – Group Projects:** A student who prefers to take the lead assumes everyone else also wants significant responsibilities, according to the Golden Rule → so this student distributes tasks equally, regardless of members' strengths and preferences. *(Error: imposing one's own preferences on others instead of considering their actual needs.)*
- **Example 2 – Lending Money:** A generous student thinks that lending money without expecting it back is a kind gesture, in line with the Golden Rule → then starts borrowing money from classmates and doesn't think about returning it. *(Error: misapplying the rule — one's own good behavior doesn't justify taking advantage of others' kindness.)*

**Conclusion (the correct takeaway):**
> The Golden Rule does **not** mean treating others exactly as you would want to be treated, but rather **considering their personal preferences and feelings**.

> 💡 **Review tip:** This is a critical-thinking exercise — practice spotting logical fallacies in a text that sounds reasonable but actually misapplies a classic ethical principle.

**Question 4: Should robots be allowed to make life-or-death decisions?**

**Question 5: Would you use an AI that reads all your private messages to keep you safe?**

> 💡 **Review tip — key AI Ethics topics to know:**
> - Trade-off between **privacy vs. public safety**.
> - The issue of **AI replacing jobs** — efficiency benefits vs. social impact.
> - **Responsibility/ethics of delegating decision-making power** to AI/robots (especially life-or-death decisions).
> - **Consent** when AI collects/uses personal data.
