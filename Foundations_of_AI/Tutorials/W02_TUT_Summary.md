## 2. Explore & Share Your Dream AI Application

### Applications of AI
The slide shows illustrations of AI applications across various fields.

### Group Activity
1. **[5 mins]** Tutor divides the class into 3–4 groups.
2. **[15 mins]** Pick an (existing or imagined) AI application you want to share. Discuss the following questions with your group:
   - What is the initial problem?
   - How does AI specifically address the problem? What type(s)/algorithm(s) of AI are used (e.g., machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, generative AI, limited memory, reactive machine,...)?
   - Are there any issues or pitfalls associated with using AI in this application?
3. **[15 mins]** Prepare a 3-minute slide or infographic using **canva.com**, then post the link to this Padlet:
   `https://rmit.padlet.org/binhnguyen49/fascinating-ai-applications-worldwide-xuwg30bww55mpe68`
4. **[5 mins per group]** Present and Q&A.

### Worked Example: Self-driving Cars
- **Description:** True self-driving cars (level 5) let the AI drive the car entirely on its own (no human assistance during the driving task at any time).
- **Initial problem:** Safety, transportation cost-effectiveness, accessibility for disabled people.
- **How AI addresses the problem:** AI acts as the "brain" of the car by processing environmental data collected by sensors such as radars and cameras. The AI performs all driving tasks such as lane keeping, accelerating, decelerating, maintaining distance, and stopping. It uses many types of AI algorithms: deep learning, reinforcement learning, generative AI...
- **Pitfalls:** Job loss for drivers, high car prices, responsibility issues (determining guilt in accidents), threat of terrorist/hacker misuse.
