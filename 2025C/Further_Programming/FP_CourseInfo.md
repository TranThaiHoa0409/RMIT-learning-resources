## Learning Outcomes

- Understand about advanced OO concepts and be able to design as well as to implement them.
- Understand about client-server architecture and parallelism using multi-threading.
- Understand about OO design patterns and their applications.

## Attendance Policies

- Although I do not take attendance, I expect no one to be absent. It will not affect your grades that you do not show up to the class, but it might not help you in assessing your work should you be on the borderline between different grades (e.g., CR-DI). Think about it. How would I be able to reconsider the hardwork you have done if I have no impression of you?

## Policy on Late Assignments

- Late assignments will be accepted for no penalty if a valid excuse is communicated to the instructor, at least 1 day, before the deadline. For example, if you sent an email asking for a slight extension 1 hour before the deadline, there is a good chance the teaching team does not have enough time to attend to and/or review your request.
- After the deadline, assignments will be accepted for a 50% deduction to the score up to 5 days after the deadline. After this any assignment handed in will be given 0.
