## What Will You Be Able to Do After This Course?

- Demonstrate a **comprehensive knowledge** of the fundamental concepts of programming.
- Design **algorithmic solutions** to a specific problem.
- Create **programming solutions** for small to medium-scale practical problems.
- Demonstrate good **software development practices**.
- Demonstrate skills for **self-directed learning**.

## Course Structure

**12 workshops · 5 sets of assessments**

| Week | Module | Assessments |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Week 1: Problem Solving and Algorithms | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 1 |
| 2 | Week 2: Variables and Assignments | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 2; In-class Quiz 1 |
| 3 | Week 3: Math methods / Input & Output | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 3 |
| 4 | Week 4: Branches | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 4; In-class Quiz 2; In-class Programming Challenge 1 |
| 5 | Week 5: Loops | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 5 |
| 6 | Week 6: Array | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 6; In-class Quiz 3 |
| 7 | Week 7: User-Defined Methods | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 7 |
| 8 | Week 8: Objects and Classes | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 8; In-class Quiz 4; In-class Programming Challenge 2 |
| 9 | Week 9: University Holidays (no class) | — |
| 10 | Day 10: Objects and Inheritance | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 10 |
| 11 | Day 11: Searching and Sorting Algorithms | Self-directed Progress Activities for Week 11; In-class Quiz 5 |

### Activities for One Workshop

- **Warm up Video**: a quick introductory overview of the concepts to be covered. Finish all before the workshop.
- **Warm up reading**: reading material is embedded inside each module. Finish all before the workshop.
- **Labs**: practical exercises. Finish some before the workshop.

## General Advice

- **Finish** warm-up readings and labs.
- **Ask questions** for clarification.
- Attempt as many **optional labs** as possible.
- Respect RMIT student conduct regulations and academic integrity (link in Further Reading).
- **Use English** in class and whenever a foreign friend joins the meeting.

## Learning at Home
